# Apple demo research and blueprint

Status: user approved the slice and story direction. The v0.2 generator and validation are implemented; generation-bundle.json and generation-config.json contain the completed implementation contract. The original bundle.json retains the earlier review packet. See ../../docs/generation.md.

Target audience: commercial finance leadership and CRO. Proposed slice: 50,000 lines over January 2024–June 2026, 300 fictional business accounts with 2–5 purchasers each, iPhone/MacBook Air/iPad Air, and US/UK/India/mainland-China markets. Apple regions guide rollups; internal territories and operational detail are synthetic.

Research uses public Apple disclosures read on October 28, 2026. This was an authoring-session web research pass, not an execution of the live-agent adapter or independent agent evaluation. Bundle source excerpts are short locators; original URLs and table locations support the full claims.

Run `PYTHONPATH=src python -m bravura validate examples/apple/bundle.json` and `PYTHONPATH=src python -m bravura render examples/apple/bundle.json --out outputs/apple-review-new`.

The packet proposes three simulated mechanisms: growth with contribution pressure, account decline with partial explanation, and mixed promotional economics. Public news motivates relevance but does not prove their effects. Latest Q3 FY2026 growth is retained to avoid presenting FY2025 regional softness as a current universal trend.

Two explicit dates: operational history ends June 30, 2026; authoring research cutoff is October 28, 2026. No later-published fact is backdated as operational evidence. Calendar YoY differs from Apple fiscal-year reporting. Before generation, validate each product version's availability and freeze executable scenario bounds and financial parameters. This is a reviewable direction, not an approved generation specification.
