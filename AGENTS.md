# Bravura working rules

Read the README, research-led strategy, implementation plan, and narrative playbook before changing generation behavior.

- Preserve working behavior and make the smallest coherent change.
- Label verified behavior, implemented but unverified behavior, proposals, and gaps separately.
- Generate business mechanisms and dependent records before deriving outcomes.
- Use deterministic arithmetic for financial measures; missing cost is unknown, not zero.
- Keep revenue, gross profit, contribution, modeled opportunity, approved action value, and realized benefit distinct.
- Attach population, period, metric, and comparison basis to every quantitative finding. Year-over-year and peer differences are not causal effects.
- Validate the final combined dataset, including shared populations and interaction residuals.
- Keep generated output out of Git. Never commit credentials.
- Answer keys, counterfactuals, generators, authoring documents, and historical findings must not enter blind analyst exports.
- Separate folders are not access control. Blind evaluations need an isolated environment and negative access tests.
- Never claim a historical Signal test was rerun unless it actually was. Do not adopt Signal's orchestration framework or industry assumptions by default.
- Do not describe fresh-context evaluation as complete without a frozen analyst result and evidence of enforced isolation.

- Research the organization before designing a demo. Use a recognizable slice by default and target tens of thousands of order lines.
- Declare dimensions, hierarchy relationships, keys, grain and effective dates before generating facts. Keep customers separate from their multiple purchasers.
- Review the proposed slice, dimension blueprint and stories together before generation. Label reported facts, inferences, unknowns and synthetic assumptions.

- Require at least two full years of transaction history. Design for comparable YoY periods, explicitly separating new/lost entities and fiscal-calendar differences. YoY observations are not causal effects.
